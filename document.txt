2. PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================
2.1 Problem Statement
Autonomous vehicle platooning is a coordinated multi-vehicle driving paradigm
where a group of Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs) travel closely
together at synchronized velocities. While classical platooning relies on fixed
linear controllers (such as PID or Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control / CACC),
real-world transportation networks introduce complex dynamic uncertainties:
  1. Abrupt weather variations (rain, fog, overcast) altering tire-road surface friction.
  2. Non-communicating, unpredictable human-driven traffic (aggressive, conservative,
     distracted, or erratic drivers).
  3. Dynamic traffic signal states (red, yellow, green) at urban intersections.
  4. Rogue or anomalous traffic behaviors ("Ghost Driver" cut-ins and lane intrusions).

Classical fixed-rule controllers struggle to adapt when friction drops by 60%
or when background vehicles cut into the platoon gap. Conversely, single-agent
reinforcement learning fails to capture the multi-agent cooperative dynamics of
a vehicle cohort.

2.2 Solution Approach
This project develops an end-to-end intelligent platooning system combining:
  - High-fidelity physical simulation in CARLA Simulator 0.9.9.
  - Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization (MAPPO) operating under the
    Centralized Training with Decentralized Execution (CTDE) framework.
  - A 32-dimensional local observation vector per vehicle encoding ego kinematics,
    predecessor headway, leader distance, traffic lights, weather friction,
    corridor geometry, and surrounding vehicle awareness.
  - A decoupled hierarchical control architecture: high-rate (30 Hz) low-level
    safety and route-following physics controller, orchestrated by a tactical
    (15 Hz) discrete MAPPO policy.
  - Active environmental uncertainties: dynamic weather presets, friction managers,
    and a stateful Ghost Driver disturbance generator.

2.3 What MAPPO Contributes
MAPPO enables collective policy learning:
  - Parameter Sharing: A single shared Actor network is utilized by every CAV in
    the platoon, ensuring scalable execution regardless of platoon size.
  - Decentralized Execution: Each CAV feeds its own local 32-D observation into
    the shared policy, yielding individualized tactical decisions (e.g., Leader
    cruises or accelerates while Follower 1 decelerates to maintain headway).
  - Centralized Critic: A centralized value network observes the concatenated
    global state of all CAVs during training, correctly attributing collective
    platoon rewards and reducing multi-agent gradient variance.

2.4 Current State vs Future Modules
  - Module 1 (Completed): Environmental Uncertainty Framework & Realistic World
    Simulation (weather shifts, friction changes, driver profiles, ghost disturbances).
  - Module 2 (Completed & Verified Working): MAPPO tactical multi-agent decision
    engine, 32-D observation pipeline, action manager with hysteresis, centralized
    critic, rollout buffer, reward manager, route-following controller, and CARLA
    synchronous GUI execution.
  - Module 3 & Future Work (Planned): Inter-platoon V2V communication network
    simulation (packet drop, transmission latency), higher-order lane merges,
    multi-platoon cooperative intersections, and physical hardware deployment.

4. OVERALL SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
The architecture adopts a dual-rate hierarchical control design that cleanly
separates tactical RL policy decisions from real-time physical vehicle stabilization.

Data and Control Flow Diagram:

   CARLA Environment (0.9.9 Unreal Engine Server, 30 Hz Sync Tick)
             │
             ▼
   Sensor & World State (Locations, Velocities, Traffic Lights, Actors, Weather)
             │
             ▼
   ObservationBuilder (observation_builder.py)
   Generates Local Observation (32-D) for each CAV + Global State (128-D)
             │
             ▼
   Shared MAPPO Actor (actor.py)
   Evaluates each vehicle's 32-D vector independently (with action masking)
             │
             ▼
   Individual Tactical Actions (0:CRUISE, 1:ACCEL, 2:DECEL, 3:LEFT, 4:RIGHT)
             │
             ▼
   ActionManager (action_manager.py)
   Applies Hysteresis (2-step persistence) & Follower Lane-Change Masking
             │
             ▼
   MissionPlatoonController (mission_controller.py)
   Evaluates Route Geometry, Traffic Light Constraints, Obstacles, Headway Gap
             │
             ▼
   CARLA VehicleControl Commands (Throttle [0, 0.65], Brake [0, 1.0], Steer [-0.65, 0.65])
             │
             ▼
   Vehicle Actuation in CARLA Simulation World
             │
             ▼
   RewardManager (reward_manager.py)
   Computes individual & collective rewards (Headway, Light, Speed, Safety, Progress)
             │
             ▼
   RolloutBuffer (buffer.py)
   Stores transitions (obs, global_state, action, log_prob, reward, value, done)
             │
             ▼
   Centralized Critic & MAPPO Trainer (trainer.py, critic.py)
   Computes GAE Advantages and updates Actor + Critic parameters via PPO

6.2 Decentralized Execution Principle
The system adheres to: "One Shared Brain, Multiple Individual Decisions."
  - Every vehicle is treated as an autonomous agent.
  - Each CAV queries the CARLA world at its own position, generating its own unique 32-D vector.
  - All vectors are passed in a batch to the Shared Actor network.
  - The network outputs distinct action logits for each CAV based on its unique state.
  - Predecessor and leader distance calculations are strictly causal: Follower 1 monitors Leader,
    Follower 2 monitors Follower 1, etc.

7. ROUTE PLANNING AND ROUTE FOLLOWING
================================================================================
7.1 Mission Route Generation
Route planning is handled by the `RoutePlanner` class in `Module2/mission_controller.py`:
  - At simulation start, the leader's spawn location is projected onto the driving road network.
  - Waypoints are sampled at a fixed spacing of `spacing_m = 2.0` meters for a mission
    distance of `distance_m = 150.0` meters (producing 76 ordered waypoints in Town01).
  - Discontinuities or cross-lane jumps are prevented during generation by minimizing:
      (wp.road_id != current.road_id, abs(wp.lane_id - current.lane_id), abs(yaw_diff))

7.2 The Route-Progress Bug and Its Resolution
  * The Bug:
    In earlier iterations, the vehicle route cursor was calculated by naively searching
    for the geometrically nearest waypoint across the entire 76-waypoint route list.
    When Town01 roads curved back near the spawn point, or at tight intersections,
    distant future waypoints (e.g., waypoint 70) were physically closer in Euclidean
    space (e.g., across a building or barrier) than the next road waypoint.
    This caused the cursor to jump instantly from index 2 to index 74.
    Once `cursor >= len(route) - 3`, the controller triggered `destination_complete`
    override, setting `target_speed = 0.0` and permanently stopping vehicles right
    after spawn.
  * The Fix:
    The cursor tracking logic was completely rewritten with three strict constraints:
      1. Bounded Advancement: The cursor cannot advance by more than `max_advance = 3`
         waypoints per tick.
      2. Longitudinal Forward Projection: The vehicle must physically advance past the
         segment's midpoint along the tangent vector:
         s_proj = (loc - wp_curr) · (wp_next - wp_curr) / |seg|
      3. Lateral Corridor Constraint: Waypoint updates are ignored if the vehicle is
         outside the lateral road corridor (`max_lateral = 4.5m`).
      4. Monotonic Cursor: A vehicle's cursor is strictly non-decreasing (`cursor >= last`).
    This fix was validated with 5 unit tests in `test_module2_logic.py` and confirmed
    in CARLA simulation where vehicles continuously traverse the entire route without stalling.

7.3 Lateral Control (Pure Pursuit) and Coordinate Geometry
Lateral vehicle steering uses Pure Pursuit geometry:
  - Lookahead Distance: `Ld = clamp(5.0 + 0.7 * speed, 6.0, 16.0)` meters.
  - Target waypoint selected along the planned route at distance `Ld`.
  - Vehicle transform defines ego coordinates:
      dx = target.x - tf.location.x
      dy = target.y - tf.location.y
      local_x =  dx * cos(yaw) + dy * sin(yaw)
      local_y = -dx * sin(yaw) + dy * cos(yaw)
      alpha = atan2(local_y, max(1.0, local_x))
  - CARLA UE4 Left-Handed Coordinate Convention:
      In CARLA/Unreal Engine, +X is Forward, +Y is Right, and +Z is Up.
      Steering in CARLA `VehicleControl`:
        +1.0 = Maximum Right Turn
        -1.0 = Maximum Left Turn
      When target is to the right, `local_y > 0` and `alpha > 0`.
      Steering formula:
        raw_steer = atan2(2.7 * sin(alpha), lookahead) * 1.8
      (Note: An inverted minus sign in prior code that caused vehicles to veer into curbs
      was identified and permanently fixed; lateral error dropped to 0.00m in live runs).
  - Steer Smoothing:
      steer = clamp(previous_steer * 0.78 + raw_steer * 0.22, previous - 0.08, previous + 0.08)
      Clamped to [-0.65, 0.65].

8. MAPPO ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
8.1 Shared Actor Network (`SharedActor` in `actor.py`)
  - Input Dimension: 32 (Local Observation Vector).
  - Architecture: Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) with 2 hidden layers:
      * Linear(32 -> 64) -> Tanh()
      * Linear(64 -> 64) -> Tanh()
      * Linear(64 -> 5) -> Action Logits
  - Action Masking:
      * For Leader: All 5 actions permitted.
      * For Followers: Actions 3 (CHANGE_LEFT) and 4 (CHANGE_RIGHT) are masked
        out by setting their logits to -1e9 before softmax.
  - Sampling:
      * Training: `torch.distributions.Categorical(logits=logits).sample()`
      * Evaluation: `torch.argmax(logits, dim=-1)` (Deterministic Greedy Action).

8.2 Centralized Critic Network (`CentralizedCritic` in `critic.py`)
  - Input Dimension: 128 (Global Centralized State = 32 dimensions * MAX_PLATOON_SIZE 4).
  - Architecture:
      * Linear(128 -> 128) -> Tanh()
      * Linear(128 -> 64) -> Tanh()
      * Linear(64 -> 1) -> Scalar State-Value Estimate V(s)
  - Purpose: Evaluates the joint value of the entire platoon configuration to guide
    actor policy gradient updates without being constrained by partial local observations.

8.3 Centralized Training with Decentralized Execution (CTDE)
  - Training Time: The centralized critic has access to the full 128-D global state
    combining all vehicles' observations. Advantage estimation uses generalized
    advantage estimation (GAE) across the collective state.
  - Execution Time: The critic is completely discarded. Each vehicle uses ONLY its
    local 32-D observation and the shared actor weights to select actions independently.

10. ACTION SPACE
================================================================================
10.1 Tactical Action Mapping
The MAPPO Actor outputs one discrete categorical action from 5 possible classes:

  Action ID | Tactical Name | Nominal Target Delta | Permitted Roles
  ----------+---------------+----------------------+------------------------
  0         | CRUISE        | 0.0 m/s (Maintain)   | Leader & Followers
  1         | ACCELERATE    | +1.1 m/s (+4.0 km/h) | Leader & Followers
  2         | DECELERATE    | -1.8 m/s (-6.5 km/h) | Leader & Followers
  3         | CHANGE_LEFT   | Request left lane    | Leader Only (Masked for F1..FN)
  4         | CHANGE_RIGHT  | Request right lane   | Leader Only (Masked for F1..FN)

10.2 Follower Action Masking & Remapping
Followers are structurally prohibited from independent lane changes to preserve
cohort cohesion. In `ActionManager._normalize_raw_action` and in `SharedActor._masked_logits`,
follower lane-change actions are strictly masked and converted to CRUISE.

10.3 Action Persistence and Hysteresis
To prevent rapid high-frequency control chatter (e.g., alternating ACCELERATE /
DECELERATE every frame), the `ActionManager` enforces hysteresis:
  - Constant: `config.ACTION_HYSTERESIS_STEPS = 2`.
  - A newly proposed tactical action must be requested for at least two consecutive
    15 Hz decision boundaries before the effective action sent to the vehicle
    controller transitions.

11. SHARED POLICY AND INDIVIDUAL ACTIONS
================================================================================
A central architectural advantage of this system is that all vehicles share a
single set of Actor network weights, yet execute fully decentralized, individual
actions:

     Vehicle 0 (Leader) Observation (32-D) ──┐
                                             │
     Vehicle 1 (Follower) Observation (32-D) ┼──► Shared Actor (MLP) ──► Action 0, Action 1, ...
                                             │
     Vehicle 2 (Follower) Observation (32-D) ──┘

Why Decisions Differ:
  - Vehicle 0 has `is_leader = 1.0`, `clearance = 50.0m`, `tl_state = Red@7m`.
    Its evaluation yields: DECELERATE to stop at the red light.
  - Vehicle 1 has `is_leader = 0.0`, `clearance = 11.0m`, `pred_speed = 15 km/h`.
    Its evaluation yields: CRUISE or DECELERATE matched to headway.
Because their local observations differ significantly in headway, velocity, and
role identification, the shared policy naturally outputs individual, role-appropriate
commands without requiring distinct networks for each vehicle.


================================================================================
12. REWARD FUNCTION
================================================================================
The reward system in `Module2/reward/reward_manager.py` computes both individual
agent rewards and a collective platoon synchronization term.

12.1 Reward Components and Weights:
  1. Collision Penalty:
     - Weight: `config.COLLISION_PENALTY = -50.0`
     - Condition: Follower bumper-to-bumper clearance <= 0.0 meters.
  2. Red Light Violation Penalty:
     - Weight: `config.RED_LIGHT_PENALTY = -20.0`
     - Condition: Vehicle is at a traffic light, state is RED, and speed > 0.5 m/s.
  3. Unsafe Headway Penalty:
     - Weight: `config.UNSAFE_HEADWAY_PENALTY = -2.0`
     - Condition: Follower clearance < 0.5 * desired_headway.
  4. Good Headway Reward:
     - Weight: `config.GOOD_HEADWAY_REWARD = +0.5`
     - Condition: abs(clearance - desired_headway) <= config.HEADWAY_DEADBAND (2.0m).
  5. Excessive Headway Penalty:
     - Penalty: -0.5
     - Condition: clearance > 1.5 * desired_headway (discourages platoon disintegration).
  6. Cruise Efficiency Reward:
     - Weight: `config.EFFICIENCY_REWARD = +0.1`
     - Condition: Vehicle speed > 5.0 m/s and light is not RED.
  7. Route Progress Reward:
     - Formula: min(0.2, max(0.0, route_progress * 0.2))
     - Condition: Rewards monotonic forward advancement along the planned road corridor.
  8. Safety Override Penalty:
     - Penalty: -0.02
     - Condition: Applied if low-level safety layer had to intervene and override the action.

12.2 Collective Platoon Reward Coupling:
To promote cooperative behavior over greedy individual actions, individual rewards
are blended with the platoon average:
  collective_reward = mean(individual_rewards)
  final_reward[i] = individual_reward[i] + config.COLLECTIVE_REWARD_WEIGHT * collective_reward
  (where `COLLECTIVE_REWARD_WEIGHT = 0.5`).

13. TRAINING PIPELINE
================================================================================
13.1 Hyperparameters (`Module2/config.py`)
  - Decision Extraction Rate: 15.0 Hz (STATE_EXTRACTION_INTERVAL = 0.0667s).
  - Physics Control Step: 30.0 Hz (CONTROL_DT = 0.0333s).
  - Rollout Length: 128 tactical decision steps per update.
  - Actor Learning Rate: 3e-4 (Adam Optimizer).
  - Critic Learning Rate: 1e-3 (Adam Optimizer).
  - Discount Factor (Gamma): 0.99.
  - GAE Lambda: 0.95.
  - PPO Clip Range (Epsilon): 0.2.
  - Entropy Regularization: 0.01.
  - Value Loss Coefficient: 0.5.
  - Max Gradient Norm: 0.5 (gradient clipping enabled).
  - PPO Optimization Epochs (K_EPOCHS): 4 epochs per rollout buffer flush.
  - Minibatch Size: 64 samples.

13.2 Training Execution Flow
  1. During simulation, every 0.0667s the manager collects observations from all CAVs.
  2. Actions and values are sampled and cached.
  3. After CARLA executes the control step, rewards and terminal states are computed.
  4. Transitions are pushed into the `RolloutBuffer`.
  5. When buffer size reaches 128 transitions, `trainer.train()` executes:
       - Computes Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE) across the stored trajectory.
       - Normalizes advantages across the batch.
       - Runs 4 PPO epochs using minibatches of 64.
       - Minimizes combined Loss = L_CLIP + 0.5 * L_VF - 0.01 * Entropy.
       - Increments `update_step`.
       - Automatically serializes the new weights to disk.

14.2 Dimension Validation and Elimination of Silent Failures
In older revisions, legacy checkpoints trained on a 14-dimensional observation
schema failed silently upon loading, leaving the networks initialized with random
weights without alerting the user.
The current loader in `trainer.py` explicitly validates dimensions:
  - If `actor_state['net.0.weight'].shape[1] != 32`, loading fails with an explicit
    `ValueError` logging:
    "Incompatible actor observation dimension: checkpoint has 14 dims, but current model requires 32 dims."
  - In `evaluate` mode, if a valid compatible checkpoint cannot be loaded, execution
    immediately raises a `RuntimeError` rather than silently degrading to random actions.

15. DETERMINISTIC EVALUATION
================================================================================
The system supports two distinct operational modes controlled via `--mode`:
  1. `train` (Default during learning):
     - Uses stochastic action sampling: Categorical(logits).sample().
     - Gathers transitions into RolloutBuffer and updates neural network weights.
     - Saves checkpoints periodically.
  2. `evaluate` (Used for demos and reproducible evaluation):
     - Uses deterministic greedy action selection: argmax(logits, dim=-1).
     - Buffer storage and gradient updates are disabled.
     - Weights remain strictly frozen.
     - Guarantees predictable, repeatable demonstration behavior.


================================================================================
16. ACTION -> VEHICLE CONTROL PIPELINE
================================================================================
A high-level discrete RL action must never be mapped directly to raw vehicle pedals,
as doing so causes erratic vehicle shaking. The hierarchical translation operates as follows:

  1. MAPPO Action Selection:
     Policy outputs discrete action (e.g., 2: DECELERATE).
  2. Action Manager Processing:
     Hysteresis confirms action consistency over 2 decision frames.
  3. Target Speed Modulation:
     Base cruise speed = `platoon_speed` (e.g., 28.0 km/h = 7.78 m/s).
     Modulation:
       CRUISE: target_speed = base
       ACCELERATE: target_speed = base + 1.1 m/s
       DECELERATE: target_speed = base - 1.8 m/s
  4. Friction Adaptation:
     target_speed = target_speed * (0.65 + 0.35 * friction)
  5. Longitudinal Headway Clamping:
     Followers dynamically bound target_speed:
       if gap < desired_headway: target = min(target, pred_speed - delta)
  6. Traffic Light & Safety Override:
     If approaching RED light (<35m), target_speed scales down to 0.0 m/s.
  7. Pedal Error Control:
     error = target_speed - current_speed
     throttle = clamp(0.23 * error, 0.0, 0.65)
     brake    = clamp(-0.42 * error, 0.0, 1.0)
     If brake > 0.02, throttle is forced to 0.0.
  8. Actuation:
     Applied to CARLA via `carla.VehicleControl(throttle=..., brake=..., steer=...)`.

17. TRAFFIC LIGHT HANDLING
================================================================================
Traffic lights in CARLA are fully integrated into both observation and control:
  - Sensing: Queries CARLA native actor API (`vehicle.get_traffic_light()` and
    `vehicle.get_traffic_light_state()`).
  - Observation Representation:
      * Feature 14: Signal state (1.0 for Red, 0.5 for Yellow, 0.0 for Green/None).
      * Feature 15: Binary indicator if vehicle is inside the intersection trigger box.
      * Feature 16: Normalized distance to the light actor.
  - Controller Enforcement:
      * When signal is RED and vehicle is within 35 meters:
        stopping_distance = max(0.0, light_distance - 4.0)
        target_speed = min(target_speed, sqrt(2.0 * 3.0 * friction * stopping_distance))
      * Within 8 meters of red stop line: brake forced to >= 0.35 if moving.
  - Demonstration Result: Verified in Town01 where the Leader approached a RED light
    at 7.2 meters away, smoothly decelerated from 16 km/h to 0.9 km/h, and brought
    the entire platoon to a controlled stop before the intersection line.

19. WEATHER / ROAD FRICTION
================================================================================
19.1 Friction Model
Road weather conditions directly govern tire-road friction coefficients (mu):
  - CLEAR: mu = 1.0 (Dry asphalt baseline)
  - CLOUDY: mu = 0.9
  - LIGHT_RAIN: mu = 0.6
  - MODERATE_RAIN: mu = 0.6
  - HEAVY_RAIN: mu = 0.4 (Slippery wet asphalt)
  - FOG: mu = 0.7
  - RAIN_FOG: mu = 0.4

19.2 Real-Time Physical Application
  - CARLA Wheels: Applied directly to vehicle physical wheels via CARLA friction API
    (`WheelPhysicsControl(tire_friction=mu)`).
  - Observation: Normal tire friction coefficient is placed into Feature 17 of the 32-D observation.
  - Controller Adaptation: Target headway increases automatically when friction drops:
      desired_headway = base (7.5m) + 0.5 * speed + (1.0 - friction) * 10.0
    Under Heavy Rain (friction = 0.4), the safety headway expands by +6.0 meters.


================================================================================
20. SAFETY SYSTEM
================================================================================
The project follows the strict design philosophy:
"MAPPO decides what it wants to do; the safety layer prevents unsafe execution."

Safety Guard Hierarchy:
  1. Forward Collision Avoidance:
     `_closest_forward_obstacle` detects any vehicle or obstacle in the forward corridor.
     If clearance < 3.0 meters, target speed is hard-clamped to 0.0 m/s with emergency braking.
  2. Follower Safe-Stopping Floor:
     When predecessor vehicle slows below 1.0 m/s (e.g. at red light), the follower
     switches from linear headway tracking to parabolic stop-distance deceleration:
       stop_dist = max(0.0, gap - 5.0)
       target = min(target, sqrt(2.0 * 2.5 * stop_dist))
     If gap drops below 5.5m while moving, brake is clamped to >= 0.60; if gap < 4.2m,
     full emergency brake (1.0) is triggered.
  3. Route Corridor Enforcement:
     Steering commands are strictly bounded to [-0.65, 0.65] radians, preventing sudden jackknifes.
  4. Lane Change Safety Lock:
     If a lane change is requested into an occupied lane (<15m clearance to traffic),
     the action is rejected by the safety layer (`REQUEST_REJECTED_UNSAFE`).

  The controller defines six discrete operational states:
    1. NORMAL_PLATOON (0.0): Standard cohesive platoon cruising.
    2. INTRUSION_DETECTED (0.25): An external vehicle enters the longitudinal gap between CAVs.
    3. PLATOON_SPLIT (0.50): Intrusion persists > 0.5s; platoon formally splits into sub-platoons.

    5. REJOIN_ELIGIBLE (0.80): Intruder departs; clearance > 1.25 * needed headway outside junctions.
    6. REJOINING (1.00): Rear sub-platoon accelerates to close the gap and reconstitute platoon.

23. MAJOR BUGS FOUND AND FIXED
================================================================================
Chronological summary of significant bugs discovered, diagnosed, and resolved:

Bug 1 — Route Cursor Jumping and Premature Stalling
  - Symptom: Vehicles stopped moving within 1-2 meters of spawn point.
  - Root Cause: Unbounded nearest-neighbor search along the 76-waypoint route picked
    geographically close road loops, instantly jumping the cursor to index 74 and
    triggering `destination_complete` override.
  - Fix: Implemented monotonic cursor tracking, longitudinal segment projection (`s_proj`),
    corridor reach constraints, and a maximum advancement of 3 waypoints per tick.
  - Verification: 5 unit tests added; full 150m route traversal verified in CARLA.

Bug 2 — Incompatible 14-D Historical Checkpoint Schema
  - Symptom: Model expected 32-D observations but failed to load or crashed on older weights.
  - Root Cause: Earlier prototype used a 14-feature vector. When observation space was expanded
    to 32 features, old checkpoint tensors were incompatible.
  - Fix: Checkpoint loader inspects `actor_state['net.0.weight'].shape[1]`. If not equal to 32,
    it raises an informative error and aborts.

Bug 3 — Silent Checkpoint Loading Failure in Evaluation Mode
  - Symptom: In evaluate mode, invalid checkpoints quietly defaulted to random weights.
  - Root Cause: Missing error propagation allowed `Module2Manager` to continue without valid weights.
  - Fix: Added strict enforcement in `Module2Manager.__init__`: evaluation mode raises
    a hard `RuntimeError` if a compatible checkpoint cannot be loaded.

Bug 4 — Insufficient MAPPO Training and Policy Collapse
  - Symptom: Checkpoints saved at update step 1-2 produced uniform random or single-action collapse.
  - Root Cause: Networks had not experienced sufficient rollout diversity.
  - Fix: Continued live training through multiple 128-step rollouts, advancing the verified
    checkpoint to update step 14 with balanced action distributions.

Bug 5 — Inverted Lateral Pure Pursuit Steering Sign
  - Symptom: In earlier runs, vehicles drifted into curbs and collided with walls at tick 300
    with high lateral error (`lat_err = 6.98m`).
  - Root Cause: CARLA UE4 coordinate convention is left-handed (+Y is Right). Steer in CARLA
    maps +1.0 to Right and -1.0 to Left. An extraneous negative sign in `raw_steer = -atan2(...)`
    inverted the steering, turning wheels Left when waypoints were Right.
  - Fix: Removed the negative sign: `raw_steer = atan2(2.7 * sin(alpha), lookahead) * 1.8`.
  - Verification: Lateral deviation immediately dropped to 0.00m; vehicles stay centered.

Bug 6 — Follower Low-Speed Creep at Traffic Light Stops
  - Symptom: Follower closed distance and bumped stopped leader at red lights.
  - Root Cause: When leader stopped (`pred_speed = 0`), follower braking was proportional
    only to velocity error (`-0.42 * error`), resulting in weak braking (0.38) at low speeds.
  - Fix: Added a follower safe-braking floor in `mission_controller.py`: when predecessor gap < 5.5m
    and vehicle is moving, brake is forced to >= 0.60; at < 4.2m, full emergency brake (1.0).

31.2 Root Cause Diagnosis
  1. Checkpoint Freshness & Multi-File Discrepancy:
     - Discrepancy: The user inspected D:\I FILES\Studies\sem7\MiniProj\checkpoints\mappo_checkpoint.pt
       (modification date: 07-10-2026, update_step=1).
     - Cause: config.py and module2_manager.py were saving and loading from

     - Cause: In mission_controller.py._target_speed(), follower tactical actions

       the follower reference speed: (pred_speed + action_delta + headway_bias) * friction,
       subject to safety bounds. Verified that ACCELERATE yields higher reference speeds
       than DECELERATE in unit testing.
     - Structured Decision Tracing: Every MAPPO inference generates a unique Decision ID
       (DEC-<frame>-<veh_id>-<time_ms>) passed from module2_manager to mission_controller,

  3. Traffic-Light Compliance & Stop-Line Physics:
     - Discrepancy: Dashboard previously showed Traffic Lt: RED while MAPPO displayed ACCELERATE.
     - Cause: CARLA 0.9.9 traffic light actor position (get_location()) points to the physical
       pole on the sidewalk (often lateral > 8m and offset from the road).
     - Fix: Used light.trigger_volume projected into road coordinates:
       light.get_transform().transform(light.trigger_volume.location).
     - Physics & Dilemma Zone: Implemented deceleration physics:
       stopping_dist = max(0.0, light_dist - 2.0); v_light = sqrt(2 * a_brake * friction * stopping_dist).
       For amber signals, evaluated dilemma-zone stopping requirement: d_needed = v^2 / (2a) + v * 0.35.
       If vehicle cannot stop safely without skidding at high speed, safely proceeds (AMBER_PROCEED);
       otherwise enforces yellow hard stop (YELLOW).
     - Safety Override Separation: Explicit override reasons logged:
       RED_LIGHT_STOP, AMBER_STOP, COLLISION_RISK, INSUFFICIENT_HEADWAY, LANE_CHANGE_UNSAFE.
       When signal is RED and MAPPO requests ACCELERATE, target speed is forced to 0.0,
       throttle=0.0, brake=1.0, with reason RED_LIGHT_STOP. Zero red-light crossings.
